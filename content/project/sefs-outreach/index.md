The **Scientist in Every Florida School (SEFS)** program, run through the Thompson Earth Systems Institute at the University of Florida, embeds active research scientists in K-12 classrooms. As a Smallwood Foundation Fellow (2020–2022), I was embedded in Marion and Levy County public schools as a Scientist in Residence.

## Activities

- **Classroom outreach**: Conducted curriculum-aligned science lessons with hundreds of K-12 students per year across 19+ classes
- **Teacher professional development**: Led workshops on scientific tools, the research process, and integrating science into classroom curricula
- **Community engagement**: Presented at STEM fairs, museums, and community festivals  
- **Judging & mentoring**: Reviewed student projects at regional and state science fairs (including the Florida State Science and Engineering Fair)

## Impact

- 2025: 350 students reached across 19 K-12 classes
- 2024: 225 students reached across 12 K-12 classes
- 2022: Secured continued Smallwood Foundation funding ($32,000) for program continuation
- 2021–2022: Conducted boat tours on the Silver River and field instruction at Silver Springs State Park
