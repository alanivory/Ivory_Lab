abstract: |
  Training large-scale neural networks across distributed clusters remains a significant bottleneck in modern machine learning workflows. We propose AdaptGrad, a novel adaptive gradient compression scheme that dynamically adjusts compression ratios based on gradient magnitude distributions. Our approach achieves up to 8× communication reduction with less than 0.5% accuracy loss on standard benchmarks. Experiments on ResNet-152 and GPT-2 across 64-GPU clusters demonstrate consistent wall-clock speedups of 3–5× compared to uncompressed baselines.
