abstract: |
  As machine learning tools proliferate beyond specialist communities, understanding how non-expert practitioners interact with them is critical. We conducted a qualitative user study with 32 participants from diverse backgrounds — including biologists, social scientists, and journalists — to examine pain points in existing ML toolkits. Our findings reveal three primary barriers: opacity of model outputs, difficulty diagnosing failures, and lack of domain-appropriate abstractions. We propose a set of HCI design guidelines for building more accessible ML tools and validate them in a follow-up design probe study.
