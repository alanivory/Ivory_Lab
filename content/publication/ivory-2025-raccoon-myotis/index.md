abstract: |
  Stormwater sewer systems are ubiquitous features of urban landscapes that may provide habitat for wildlife. We investigated factors influencing raccoon (Procyon lotor) and Southeastern Myotis (Myotis austroriparius) use of stormwater sewer systems in Alachua County, Florida, using camera trap and acoustic monitoring data. Our results provide insight into the ecological role of stormwater infrastructure as urban wildlife habitat.
