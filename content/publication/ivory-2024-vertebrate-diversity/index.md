abstract: |
  Urban stormwater sewer systems represent an understudied component of city infrastructure that may support surprising vertebrate diversity. We documented vertebrate species using stormwater sewer systems in Alachua County, Florida, recording species including alligators, raccoons, and bats. This study highlights the ecological importance of stormwater infrastructure and has implications for urban wildlife management and infrastructure design.
