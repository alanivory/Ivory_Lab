abstract: |
  Woody plant encroachment and forest densification are widespread phenomena across the Eastern United States. We analyzed regional drivers of woody cover change, distinguishing between forest infilling — the thickening of existing forest — and land-use conversion from open habitats to woody cover. Our findings reveal distinct regional patterns with implications for wildfire risk, biodiversity, and carbon cycling in eastern forests.
