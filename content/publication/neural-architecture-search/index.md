abstract: |
  Neural Architecture Search (NAS) has enabled automatic discovery of high-performing model architectures, yet existing methods remain computationally expensive. We introduce DiffPrune-NAS, a differentiable pruning framework that jointly learns architecture parameters and pruning masks in a single training pass. Our method reduces the search cost by 40× compared to DARTS while achieving competitive performance on CIFAR-10 and ImageNet. We further demonstrate that the discovered architectures transfer effectively to downstream tasks, outperforming hand-designed networks by up to 2.1% top-1 accuracy.
