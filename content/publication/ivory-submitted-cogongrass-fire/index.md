abstract: |
  Cogongrass (Imperata cylindrica) is among the most invasive plants in the southeastern United States and is known to alter fire behavior. We modeled cogongrass impacts on fire behavior across environmental and seasonal gradients using field measurements, remote sensing, and fire behavior modeling. Results reveal how this invasive grass increases fire intensity and spread rate, with implications for wildfire risk management across southeastern forests.
