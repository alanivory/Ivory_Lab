abstract: |
  K-12 science outreach programs that pair active research scientists with classroom teachers offer a powerful model for improving science education. We describe the Scientist in Every Florida School (SEFS) program, which embeds graduate student scientists into K-12 schools to co-develop and deliver science lessons aligned with state curricula. We document program design, implementation, and early outcomes, providing a transferable framework for scientist-teacher partnership programs.
